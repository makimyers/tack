# Review a Playcap session

1. Open the exact local `session.md` the player shared. If they ask for the latest and the `latest` pointer is stale, pick the newest completed report under that game's `playtests/` and name it. Use the report's recorded worktree and revision when identifying the tested build. Treat older reports without a revision as unidentified builds.
2. Read the timeline for the player's own observations. Open the relevant `moments/<n>.jpg`. Use the corresponding original frames or reel pages if text, motion or timing is unclear. Check the few seconds before each remark because the event may precede the comment. Split recordings and missing audio can make alignment approximate.
3. Give the player up to three actionable findings in priority order. For each, cite elapsed play time, the remark or observed frame, the likely issue, and the smallest useful change or verification. Distinguish what the player reported from what you observed or inferred. A silent capture can verify visible behavior but offers no evidence of how it felt. If the packet lacks enough evidence, say what is missing.
4. End with one next fix or one specific observation to collect. A request to review calls for findings. The player chooses when to record and whether to implement a fix. Keep audio and captured files local unless the player asks to share them.

Use the game-studio visual critic for matched before-and-after views of proposed changes. Use this skill for the player's recorded session.
